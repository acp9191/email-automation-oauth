Personal Email Automation

Personal Email Automation is a private tool that one person runs on their own computer. It watches its owner's Gmail inbox for the monthly Verizon internet bill notification and forwards a copy of the bill to that owner's work email address for expense reimbursement.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Gmail access

  • Read-only access (gmail.readonly) to find the bill notification email.
  • Send-only access (gmail.send) to email the bill to a single, pre-configured address.

It cannot delete, modify, or label any message.
